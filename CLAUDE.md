## Doors — do not bundle

1. `/platform` — white-label shop: missed-call text-back, secretary, invoice follow-up, Google reviews. Any trade on the phone.
2. `/cleaning` — CrewFlag. Standing water, leftover product, unfinished rooms. Not “misses.”
3. `/shipping` — InvoiceFlag. 3PL / parcel bills. Not a cleaning tool.
4. `/premium` — PremiumFlag. A contractor’s workers comp or general liability audit bill against the payroll summary and the certificates. $249 a bill, once. Unlisted: not in the nav, footer, homepage, or About until the Stripe link exists and a paid bill has unlocked an email (`docs/premiumflag.md`, step 6).

Homepage explains the three doors. It does not sell them as a pack. PremiumFlag becomes the fourth door only at step 6.

## Language

- Cleaning: name the problem (standing water on hardwood, chemical on stone, bathroom not restocked). Do not say “catch the miss.”
- Platform: “your number, your Google listing.” Do not name HighLevel on the public site.
- InvoiceFlag: keep the live H1. No fake recovery dollars.
- PremiumFlag: the bill is the upload, not the records request. Pay or Flag, nothing softer. A line is flagged only when a document in the upload contradicts it. No promise the carrier will reduce the bill, no coverage advice, no class-code advice that is not in the documents. It does not sell insurance and it does not talk to the carrier. No fake recovery dollars.
- Plumbing is not the company. It may appear as one example trade on the platform page.
